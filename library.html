Library

The book and the tooling

Personal projects from 2025 and 2026, separate from my employment work. I built them to think through how service design methods hold up when AI agents do the first draft, and to learn what it takes to ship a tool. They were written and coded with AI assistance, and their examples use fictional organisations, labelled as such.

The book

The Agentic Service Designer, second edition. Service design foundations, methods at machine scale, and an operating model for running the practice with agents and human gates. Free to read online. The worked example throughout is a fictional ferry operator.

Method as code, with the gates written down
