# Pattern references for the workforce platform showcase

Working notes. Not published: the case study shows the product, not the homework.
One Mobbin search per component before it was drawn, two or three references read,
each reduced to what to take and what to leave. The rejection matters as much as the
reference, because most of these products are for a salaried person at a desk and
this one is for a manager on a headset between a fryer and a drive-thru window.

| Screen or component | Reference | Taken | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week roster grid | Deputy, Schedule — https://mobbin.com/screens/93321432-f493-451e-9bc7-551746341583 | Rows grouped by work area with a colour swatch per area; hours and cost on the person row; a faint plus in every empty cell; a state legend strip pinned along the bottom counting published, unpublished, open, warnings and leave | The eight-item top nav. A restaurant manager uses four things, not nine, and the other five are head office's |
| Roster warning counters | 7shifts, Schedule — https://mobbin.com/screens/cba70260-5011-4d77-abb1-e890393a4844 | Conflicts and overtime as counter pills in the header with a "Fix warnings" link beside them, so the count is a route into the work and not a badge; overtime as a pill on the person row; the whole row tinted when the person is the problem | The pale green fill on every shift. When every chip is coloured, colour stops meaning anything, and this product needs colour free to mean compliance |
| Compliance triage | Vanta, Tests — https://mobbin.com/screens/d33c50cb-a1db-4dbd-a420-49bbf678ba3d | Splitting the list into "OK" and "Needs attention" before any row is drawn, then breaking "needs attention" down by how soon it bites; an owner column, so a warning belongs to a person rather than to the screen | The automated-test framing. A break warning is not a control that passes or fails, it is money owed to somebody by Thursday |
| Severity tabs | Supabase, Advisors — https://mobbin.com/screens/e3ac7f2c-bdd0-42a4-ac13-76fb893fd06d | Errors, warnings and information as three tabs carrying their own counts, so the manager picks a severity rather than scrolling one flat list | The word "advisor". The research found managers already ignore advice; these are obligations |
| Coverage summary cards | Deel, Compliance documents — https://mobbin.com/screens/9f5af254-f966-4810-adbb-c428854523e3 | Three totals across the top, each a count over the population with a coloured dot, so the manager sees the shape before the rows | Sending a message as the only row action. The fix here is usually to change the roster, not to chase the person |
| Timesheet and punch review | Deputy, Timesheets — https://mobbin.com/screens/40b0eeda-18ea-4067-ba0f-1b757cdbe424 | Tabs carrying counts; the rostered time struck through above the actual time when they differ, so the correction is legible at a glance; an approve action on every row rather than only in bulk | Approving from a list with no reason field. The research found dismissals with no record, which is the thing being fixed |
| Pay period close | 7shifts, Pay period — https://mobbin.com/screens/b8e19a45-7433-4ecd-bf2c-6533a88daa4b | A pay period status pill and an approved-count pill side by side above the table; exceptions as an amber count pill in its own column; a single primary action to close the period | "Close pay period" as a one-way door. The whole finding is that reopening is so painful that people work around it, so the correction path had to live inside the closed period |
| Labour against forecast | 7shifts, Sales vs labor — https://mobbin.com/screens/2f7945a1-cb4f-4763-a3cc-dc997f51f5a9 | Actual drawn solid and projected drawn dashed on one axis; three figures underneath with last week beside each | The weekly frame. Managers asked for the number during the shift, so the default view is today by hour |
| Metric comparison strip | Steep, Metric — https://mobbin.com/screens/1f477d37-7951-4fb6-bf21-ccaf275da8b7 | A row of deltas under the headline number, each naming what it is compared against, so a percentage never floats without its baseline | Four comparisons. Two is what fits on a phone at arm's length |
| Report catalogue | Square, Reports — https://mobbin.com/screens/e20d0eeb-16ea-4964-b2f8-ba5528d59265 | A named list of reports down a rail, so "I did not know that report existed" stops being possible | Reports as the only destination. Area coaches wanted one row per restaurant, which is a table, not a report |
| Multi-metric report card | Fresha, Analytics — https://mobbin.com/screens/39b5ad91-5928-4e9b-b97f-2de8c9edb879 | A headline figure with an inline delta chip over a sparkline, then sub-metrics as rows with their own chips | The five-star rating row. Nothing here is rated |
| Mobile shift list | Grab Driver, Booking planner — https://mobbin.com/screens/a2a5d8de-302d-47a0-9c99-ec6ccbc0ac54 | A week of dates as a horizontal strip above the list, with the selected day filled, so the next shift is one tap from anywhere | The bare shift rows. A team member wants the punch times and the break, which is what the research asked for and what the old app hid |
| Mobile hours summary | Remote Global HR, Weekly hours — https://mobbin.com/screens/a8c9e52c-23af-40b8-8159-830b4dec9005 | Breaks, time off and worked hours broken out as labelled chips against their totals, then one total rule at the bottom | The certification paragraph. A team member checking a break on a phone is not signing anything |
