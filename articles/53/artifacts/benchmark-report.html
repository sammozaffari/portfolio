Benchmark report: floor-role programmes

Four franchisee groups had designed four different humans for the foyer, each with its own theory of where the person belongs in a self-service channel. The job was to converge them into one floor-role standard without flattening what made each work.

What the four designs share

  • Greet within seconds at the door: all four.
  • Assist without judgement at the kiosk: all four.
  • Direct cash and specific requests to the counter: three of four.
  • Clean the foyer and the high-touch surfaces as a secondary duty: three of four.
  • Roster to lunch and dinner peaks: all four.

These five behaviours became the floor-role standard: greet, assist, direct, clean and roster, with the role required during peak hours from the first day of any kiosk installation.

The failure the benchmark caught

A post-implementation phone check called the restaurants sampled for the flagship foyer programme. None had implemented it, and none had heard of it. A well-designed role is worthless without rollout, rostering and coaching. That finding, not the successes, drove the governance design: every standard paired with an interim rollout process and audit-linked enforcement.
