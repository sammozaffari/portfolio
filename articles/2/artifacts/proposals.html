Rollout

Pilots were live by close: single sign-on, open-shift hours and manager clock-in. Release communication was templated and routed through the platform user group and the franchise channels, so the rationale for the top ten travelled with the roadmap rather than behind it. The blueprint lives on as a living document with a half-yearly review, which is the cadence that keeps the model out of the bin.
