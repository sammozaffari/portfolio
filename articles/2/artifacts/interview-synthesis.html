Interview synthesis

Four methods, one service. The interviews, the role-branched survey, the restaurant visits and the support-queue analysis all landed on the same problems, which is what made each finding defensible in the vendor conversation.

Method, four converging

InterviewsSemi-structured, from team members to area coaches, IT support, people-systems and payroll
200role-branched survey responses
10restaurant visits, equity and franchise
Support queuethe mobile-app ticket cluster, read as failure demand

Each method was chosen to cover a gap in the others. The survey reached the people who were never interviewed. The visits turned reported problems into observed ones. The ticket queue showed what people gave up on and rang about. Every role was covered by at least two methods, and where a survey branch returned nothing the interview round covered the role instead.

The survey's area-coach and franchisee branches returned nothing. That was read as a sampling signal rather than a finding: those roles do not fill in surveys. The triangulation rule caught it, and the above-restaurant interview round, with a franchisee, two area coaches and payroll, covered both roles.
