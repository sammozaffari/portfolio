Persona set: three journeys through hiring

Composite characters built from the interviews and the survey. Each one is a journey the design has to survive, and a persona that does not change a design decision is cut.

The case studyPersona set

The first-job school leaver

Context. A first ever job, with the family in the loop on the paperwork. No bank account, tax number or photo identification yet.

Goals. A job close to home; a clear next step; parents satisfied the process is legitimate.

Pains. The form asks for information the family does not have yet; identity checks become slow without photo identification; the parental-consent form expires after seven days; no human anywhere in the loop.

Design response. A minimal-ask conversation that works on a phone; guided document capture; a human review lane; the parental-consent flow runs before anything else proceeds.

The returning crew member

Context. Worked in the network before, left, and is coming back, sometimes to the same franchisee group and sometimes to a different one.

Goals. Pick up where they left off; training and credentials carry over; no repeated paperwork.

Pains. Treated as a brand-new applicant; the work-rights check and the onboarding form repeated pointlessly; records never off-boarded, so the system does not know they left.

Design response. A re-hire branch at the application stage: the same franchisee group skips the work-rights check and the onboarding and transfers the record; everyone else runs the full workflow with new records.

The manager hiring inside a full day

Context. Carries recruitment inside a day of shifts, paperwork and peaks.

Goals. Fill shifts with good people, fast; admin in minutes, not hours; see candidate status without chasing.

Pains. Re-enters candidate data across systems; texts candidates to arrange interviews; discovers wrong documents at pay time; unreachable candidates are never closed off.

Design response. A review queue with an auto-scheduled interview calendar; automated offers, reminders and employee setup; unreachable candidates dispositioned after ten days without action.
