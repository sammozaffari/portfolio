Four gates that fail the build: tokens in step with their source, contrast at WCAG 2.2 AA, no undeclared component, and visual regression against a committed baseline. A service design harness for small teams (personal, 2026). Service design tooling assumes a research team. This is built for one or two people: an agent harness that takes the first pass at the work a larger team would split up, and holds the gates that stop it going wrong. 10 agent roles, 74 method skills and 31 artefact kinds compiled into 10 host packs, reachable as an MCP server of 29 tools over JSON-RPC stdio or as a CLI. Machine-checked acceptance rules on every artefact, a fact-check that replays each evidence citation, a critic gate on the write path and a CI drift check. TypeScript, about 12,500 lines across 8 packages, with a companion book and library.
